Pizza in the Oven: How fast is the area growing?

A circular pizza bakes and its radius grows at a constant rate. Related rates links dr/dt to dA/dt.

Area rate dA/dt = 2πr·dr/dt

The math

A = πr²
dA/dt = 2πr · dr/dt
At r = 50 cm, dr/dt = 0.01 cm/min:
dA/dt = 2π(50)(0.01) = π ≈ 3.1416 cm²/min
